# Contributing to Mukta Zero

Thank you for your interest in Mukta Zero.

## Contributor License Agreement Required

Mukta requires an accepted Contributor License Agreement (“CLA”) before merging an external contribution. See `CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md`.

By submitting a pull request, you represent that you have the right to submit the contribution. The contribution will not be merged until the CLA process is complete.

## Contribution Rules

- Do not submit code, documentation, datasets, prompts, tests, images, or other material that you do not have the right to contribute.
- Do not copy code from sources whose license is incompatible with Mukta Zero’s current license model.
- Identify third-party code and its license in the pull request.
- Do not submit confidential information, personal data that is not necessary and lawful to disclose, credentials, API keys, or secrets.
- Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reports rather than opening a public issue for an undisclosed security flaw.
- Contributions must preserve required copyright, SPDX, and third-party notices.

## Licensing of Contributions

Accepted contributions may be incorporated into Mukta Zero and distributed by Mukta under the repository license, commercial licenses offered by Mukta, and the applicable Change License, as described in the CLA.
