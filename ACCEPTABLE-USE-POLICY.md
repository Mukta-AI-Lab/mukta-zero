2. **Compromise systems without authorization.** Obtain or attempt unauthorized access to systems, data, accounts, or infrastructure; deploy malicious code; materially impair the security, integrity, or availability of systems you are not authorized to test.

4. **Deceive or defraud.** Operate systems for fraud, phishing, materially deceptive impersonation, or fabrication of records represented as genuine.
5. **Endanger or abuse people.** Harass, stalk, unlawfully surveil, exploit, or endanger individuals; exploit minors; or materially facilitate development of prohibited weapons or other unlawful harmful capabilities.
6. **Violate third-party rights.** Infringe intellectual property, privacy, confidentiality, or data-protection rights, including processing personal data without an appropriate legal basis where required by applicable law.
7. **Circumvent license conditions.** Remove or materially alter license notices or required attribution; misrepresent the origin of the Software; relicense covered copies or derivative works inconsistently with `LICENSE`; or structure deployments principally to evade the five-User SCALE condition.

Good-faith security research on systems you own or are expressly authorized to test, including testing of Mukta Zero itself, is not a violation of items 2 or 3 when conducted within the scope of that authorization and applicable law.
